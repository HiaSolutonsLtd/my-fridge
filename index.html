The problem

Grocery shopping runs on guesswork.

In our own research with 169 shoppers across the UK and Romania, most people are still tracking their kitchen the hard way — and it shows.

01

Duplicate purchases

Without a live view of what's already at home, it's easy to buy things you don't need — and forget the ones you do.

02

Lists on paper or spreadsheets

64.5% of the people we surveyed still keep shopping lists on paper, and most don't stick to the list once they're in store.

03

The endless "how much milk is left?" call

Meal planning for the days ahead means constantly checking in — with the fridge, or with whoever's home to look.

What it does

One app, a full kitchen inventory.

My Fridge keeps track of what you own, what's about to expire, and what a recipe still needs — so planning takes seconds, not a trip to check the fridge.

AI-powered receipt scanning

Photograph a receipt and My Fridge reads the items straight into your inventory, powered by on-device text recognition.

Live cloud inventory

Every item, quantity and expiry date is stored securely and kept in sync the moment it changes.

Recipe import

Pull in recipes from the web and see instantly which ingredients you already have — and which you're missing.

Missing-ingredient analysis

My Fridge compares a recipe against your inventory and lists exactly what to buy, and how much.

Secure account & sign-in

Built on Firebase authentication, so your kitchen data stays private and tied to your account only.

Coming next: expiry-based suggestions

We're building meal recommendations that prioritise what's about to go off, learned from your own eating habits.

How it works

From receipt to recipe in four steps.

Step 1

Scan or add items

Snap a grocery receipt or add items by hand as you unpack the shopping.

Step 2

My Fridge fills itself in

Items, quantities and expiry dates land straight in your cloud inventory.

Step 3

Bring in a recipe

Import a recipe from the web and My Fridge checks it against what you own.

Step 4

See what's missing

Get a precise shopping list for exactly what the recipe still needs.
