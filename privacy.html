My Fridge

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Hia Solutions Ltd ("we", "us", "our") operates the My Fridge mobile application (the "App"). This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and display name, used to create and secure your account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Inventory data: the food items, quantities, and expiry dates you add or import, including data captured through the receipt-scanning feature.
  • Recipe data: recipes you import or create within the App.
  • Usage data: basic technical information (such as app version and device type) used to keep the App reliable.

How we use your information

  • To operate your personal inventory and keep it in sync across your devices.
  • To match recipes against your inventory and show missing ingredients.
  • To authenticate you and protect your account.
  • To maintain and improve the App.

How your data is stored

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). Each user's inventory is kept in an isolated, access-controlled partition of the database. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Receipt scanning

The receipt-scanning feature uses on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit) to read grocery receipts you photograph. Recognised items are added to your inventory; we do not use receipt photos for any purpose beyond extracting this information.

Your choices

  • You can review and edit your inventory at any time within the App.
  • You can delete your account from the Account screen, which removes your account and associated data from our systems.
  • You can contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data.

Children's privacy

My Fridge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. We'll update the "last updated" date above when we do.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to office@herdea.co.uk.
